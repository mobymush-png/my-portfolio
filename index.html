About Me

Hello! My name is Moreblessing Musekiwa. I am an enthusiastic educator dedicated to making Computer Science engaging, accessible, and practical for high school students.

I currently work as a high school teacher at Anderson Adventist High School, where I teach Cambridge O Level Computer Science (Forms 1 to 4).

To continuously grow in my field, I am furthering my education in Computer Science at Midlands State University. My goal is to qualify for teaching Cambridge AS and A Level Computer Science and inspire the next generation of software creators and tech innovators.
